## Structured data and metadata review

No numerical catalogue-size, uptime, refund-eligibility, or automated-activation claim should be added to metadata or structured data until its evidence gate above is satisfied. Prices and the annual promotion remain configuration-backed website statements, not proof that a payment transaction can complete.
