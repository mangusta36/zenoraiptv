# Zenora IPTV Brand and Heading SEO Report

Date: 2026-11-12  
Canonical origin: `https://www.zenoratv.vip`  
Scope: 42 indexable routes, including 19 articles, plus 7 relevant noindex routes

## Outcome

The homepage now presents `Zenora IPTV — Premium IPTV Plans & Streaming` as its single rendered H1. Commercial and support hubs use the official brand where the page is specifically about the Zenora IPTV service, while setup routes and all 19 articles retain their distinct non-branded search intent.

The audit used production output served by `next start`. The exact rendered title, description, canonical, robots value, status, and H1 count for every crawled route are recorded in [`results/seo-route-inventory.csv`](results/seo-route-inventory.csv). That inventory contains 42 indexable and 7 noindex routes; every route returned HTTP 200 and every route rendered exactly one H1.
