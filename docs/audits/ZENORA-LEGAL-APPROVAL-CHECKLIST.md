# Zenora Legal Approval Checklist

Status: **owner/counsel approval required**. The four legal routes remain `noindex, follow` and excluded from the XML sitemap. Their current pages are structured issue lists, not operative policies or contracts.

## Business inputs required for every policy

- [ ] Verified legal entity/trading name, registered address, contact details, territories served, and effective date
- [ ] Authorized approver and version/change-control owner
- [ ] Reconciliation with actual checkout, WhatsApp, support, hosting, analytics, payment, and fulfillment processes
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific counsel review and approval to publish/index

## Privacy policy

- [ ] Controller/business identity and privacy contact
- [ ] Data categories, sources, required/optional fields, purposes, and legal bases
- [ ] Processors/vendors, recipients, international transfers, and safeguards
- [ ] Retention periods and deletion rules for each data category
- [ ] User rights, identity verification, complaint route, security practices, and children’s-data position

## Terms and conditions

- [ ] Eligibility, territory, service scope, account responsibilities, acceptable use, and availability limits
- [ ] Authorized billing, renewal, cancellation, trial, suspension, termination, and service-change terms
- [ ] Reconciliation with every configured price, connection option, and the 12 paid months + 2 free months promotion
- [ ] Intellectual-property/content-rights language, disclaimers, liability, dispute process, governing law, and venue

## Refund policy

- [ ] Approved eligibility and exclusions; no seven-day promise is currently substantiated in the repository
- [ ] Claim deadline, troubleshooting prerequisites, required evidence, verified request channel, and escalation path
- [ ] Refund amount, partial-refund treatment, payment method, processing timeframe, and cancellation distinction
- [ ] Consistency with trial, pricing, checkout, FAQ, support copy, payment-provider rules, and local consumer law

## Cookie policy

- [ ] Production cookie/local-storage inventory: name, provider, purpose, category, duration, and first/third-party status
- [ ] Strictly necessary items separated from analytics, advertising, preference, chat, embedded-media, and payment tools
- [ ] Consent requirements by region, consent-record retention, withdrawal control, and banner behavior
- [ ] Vendor/transfer disclosures reconciled with the privacy notice and the deployed production configuration

## Release gate

Only remove `noindex` and add a legal route to the sitemap after the final text is populated from verified operations, approved by an authorized representative, tested for footer accessibility, and dated. Approval of one policy does not automatically approve the other three.
